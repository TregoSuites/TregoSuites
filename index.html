Questions

The things people ask first.

Can it be built as living space?

Yes, on request, where your local codes allow it. The standard build is a non-habitable accessory structure: an office, a suite, a studio, a cabin. A habitable build is a different animal: it changes the foundation, the insulation, the egress and the electrical, and it changes the price. It also has to be approved by your city or county before we start, not after. So raise it on the first call and we'll quote it properly.

Do I need a permit?

That depends on your city or county and sometimes on your HOA. Many jurisdictions in the area don't require one under a certain size, but it is the customer's responsibility to confirm. We'll tell you what we know about your area and what to ask them.

What kind of pad does it need?

A level gravel base is the usual answer and the cheapest one. Concrete works. Blocks work on the right ground. We'll spec it for your site before you order so there are no surprises on delivery day.

How do I get power to it?

On the Signature and Turnkey, a licensed electrician wires the building itself with a subpanel, outlets and fixtures. The feed from your house or meter out to the building is quoted separately, because it depends entirely on the distance and the route.

Can I change the size or layout?

Yes, within reason. We build from 10 × 16 up to 12 × 20. Window and door placement, a second door, extra outlets, a different roof color. All of that is normal. Tell us what you want and it shows up in the quote.

Is financing available?

We don't carry financing in-house. We can point you to a third-party lender that handles this kind of purchase, and plenty of buyers use a home equity line instead.

How long does it take?

Most units are built in two to three weeks once the order is placed, plus whatever the current queue is. Because we build indoors, weather doesn't push your date.

Can I move it later?

Yes. It sits on 4x6 pressure-treated skids, so it can be picked up and hauled again. That's part of why the small units aren't on a permanent foundation.
